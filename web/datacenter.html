What if the data center forecasts are right?

Four public forecasts of data center electricity, run through EIA's ported demand modules and an open dispatch model of EIA's power plant fleet. Precomputed, not live.

Data: U.S. Energy Information Administration, Annual Energy Outlook 2026 and the NEMS AEO2026 release; the forecasts cited above. NEMS Open is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by EIA or the forecast authors. About and credits.
